Bio

Hi, I'm Alihan Aydın. Welcome to my portfolio.

I'm a game developer building with Unity and C# since 2020 — and just as much a tools developer. Alongside shipping a broad range of games and prototypes, I design custom Unity editor tools — inspectors, editor windows and workflow utilities that remove friction from production — several of them open source on GitHub. I gravitate toward modern, performance-minded solutions — reaching for UniTask, LitMotion and Reflex over their older alternatives — with optimization always front of mind.
